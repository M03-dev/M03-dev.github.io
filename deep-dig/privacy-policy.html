Deep Dig – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Deep Dig ("the app", "we") collects and how it is used.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. We have no user accounts and no servers that receive your data.

Your game progress (coins, depth, upgrades and settings) is saved on your device; we never receive it. If you have turned on Android device backup, Android may include it in your own Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us. Otherwise it is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Game settings file: when the app starts (and when you return to it, at most every 6 hours), it downloads a small settings file from our website, hosted by GitHub Pages (m03-dev.github.io). The file adjusts game balance, such as timers and rewards. The app sends nothing about you or your game progress, but like any web request the download shows your device's IP address and basic device/browser information to GitHub, which may keep it in its server logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Reminder notifications: if you choose to turn on reminders (they are off until you say yes), the app plans a few notifications on your phone itself, for example when your mine cart is full or a daily gift is ready. They are created and shown entirely on your device: nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no push-notification service. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Reminders or in your phone's notification settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads and occasional ad breaks (rewarded interstitial ads) between layers provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the device advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region),
  • device type, operating system and app usage information related to ads.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalise ads. How Google handles this data is described in Google's policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy & ad choices in the app.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Rewarded ads are optional. Occasional ad breaks may be offered between ordinary layers after at least 6 minutes of active play and 4 completed layers, starting after depth 12. Each ad break first shows the reward and a "No thanks" button, so you can skip it. They never appear during timed boss or challenge layers or when opening or resuming the app.

4. Children

Deep Dig is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date of the last update shown above.
